# How Asa works — the short version

**The real instructions are one level up: `..\AGENTS.md`.** Read that first — it is Asa's own
Instruction for AI: the loop, what to read for which job, how things link, and the exact shape of
every file you write.

**If you can't reach it, here is the loop, in five lines:**

1. Catch up: run `asa-brief "<this project>"`, or read this folder's home note and the newest
   `.asa-log.md` line.
2. Say where things stand in three lines or fewer — lead with what needs Nico.
3. Work with him; write decisions, results and tasks down as they happen, not saved for the end.
4. Check what you wrote: `asa-check "<this project>"`.
5. Hand over: tick tasks, update `next-step` and `updated:`, close `.asa-session.md`, add one line
   to `.asa-log.md`.
