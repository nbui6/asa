> **The last column exists because a plan can be re-confirmed twice while the file the assistant
> reads every session still describes the product that was rejected.** On 2026-08-26 `CLAUDE.md`'s
> Goal still described a twelve-screen writing app, and two of its eight hard rules governed an API
> key and a module seam that the confirmed plan had cut. Both were being paid for in every session
> and neither could ever fire.
>
> **A change to the plan is a change to the operational file.** Check the Goal and the hard rules
> against the plan, tick the column, and where a rule no longer applies say so out loud rather than
> deleting it quietly — hard rules are guardrails, and removing one is the Boss's call.
